# Cheezish — Design System

## 1. Design Direction

Cheezish should feel:

- Dark
- Cinematic
- Premium
- Futuristic
- Bold
- Food-focused

The design must not feel like:

- A generic restaurant template
- A gaming website
- A neon cyberpunk interface
- A cartoon fast-food brand

The food must remain the main visual focus.

---

# 2. Visual Hierarchy

Priority order:

1. Food
2. Headline
3. Order CTA
4. Supporting information
5. Decorative effects

3D, particles and motion should support the food rather than overpower it.

---

Cheezish Gold:

#FFC928

- Important highlights

#F8F8F8

- Main headings

---

#9B9B9B

- Secondary metadata
- Supporting information

---

## Accent Red

#A51F24

Usage:
- Spicy menu labels
- Inferno burger
- Small visual accents

# 4. Approximate Color Balance

Preferred balance:

- 70% Black / Charcoal
- 20% Cream / White
- 8% Cheezish Gold
- 2% Accent Red

This is a visual guideline rather than an exact mathematical requirement.

---

# 5. Typography

## Heading Font

Preferred:

Space Grotesk

Usage:
- Hero headlines
- Section titles
- Product names
- Large visual statements

Style:
- Bold
- Clean
- Modern
- Strong
- Often uppercase

Example:

BITE INTO
ANOTHER DIMENSION

---

## Body Font

Preferred:

Inter

Usage:
- Menu descriptions
- Contact information
- Body copy
- Buttons
- Forms
- Supporting text

Body text must remain highly readable.

---

# 7. Logo Direction

Primary logo:

CHEEZISH

The logo should be a clean wordmark.

Possible subtle brand detail:

- Cheese-inspired cut
- Small melt detail
- Modified E character
- Small geometric cheese reference

Avoid:

- Cartoon burger logo
- Chef hat
- Large cheese illustration
- Fake luxury crest
- Multiple unrelated colors

---

# 8. Logo Variants

Primary Dark-Surface Logo:

Text:
#F8F8F8

#FFC928

SVG

The logo should remain sharp at all screen sizes.

# 9. Navbar

Desktop structure:

CHEEZISH | Home | Menu | About | Gallery | Contact | ORDER NOW

Requirements:

- Logo links to Home
- Transparent over the initial hero where appropriate
- May transition to a dark glass surface after scrolling
- ORDER NOW remains visually prominent
- Visible keyboard focus
- Clear hover state

Suggested navbar height:

72px–80px

---

## Scrolled Navbar

Suggested appearance:

Background:
rgba(8, 8, 8, 0.75)

Backdrop blur:
approximately 16px

Use subtle borders if needed.

Do not make the navbar visually heavy.

---

# 10. Mobile Navbar

Default:

CHEEZISH                         Menu Button

When opened:

- Home
- Menu
- About
- Gallery
- Contact
- ORDER NOW

Mobile navigation should be:

- Easy to close
- Keyboard accessible
- Large enough for touch
- Free from clipped items

- Light border

# 13. Interactive States

Every interactive component should have:

- Default
- Hover
- Focus-visible
- Active/pressed
- Disabled where applicable

Focus-visible state must be clearly noticeable.

Do not rely only on color changes that are difficult to distinguish.

# 18. Shadows

Use shadows subtly.

Suggested direction:

0 20px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)

Food cards should primarily separate through:

- Lighting
- Contrast
- Space

rather than giant shadows.

---

# 19. Gold Glow

Suggested subtle glow:

0 0 40px rgba(255,201,40,0.18)

Use only for:

- Primary CTA
- Important 3D highlight
- Special brand moments

Do not apply glow everywhere.

---

# 20. Food Photography Style

Preferred imagery:

- Close-up
- Strong food texture
- Dark environment
- Warm lighting
- Dramatic cheese
- Shallow depth of field
- Minimal background clutter

Avoid:

- Generic white-background stock food
- Poorly cut-out burgers
- Low-resolution imagery
- Visibly distorted AI food
- Copied restaurant photography

---

# 21. Gallery Style

Recommended visual mix:

- Burger close-up
- Cheese pull
- Loaded fries
- Burger side profile
- Drinks
- Packaging
- Ingredients
- Restaurant atmosphere

Gallery should feel cinematic while remaining usable.

---

# 22. 3D Visual Style

The hero 3D burger should feel:

- Premium
- Realistic
- Warm
- Heavy
- Detailed
- Food-focused

The scene should not feel like a generic 3D tech demo.

---

# 23. 3D Lighting

Recommended lighting direction:

## Key Light

Warm gold light.

Purpose:
Illuminate the main burger.

---

## Rim Light

Warm/gold edge light.

Purpose:
Separate burger from dark background.

---

## Fill Light

Subtle neutral fill.

Purpose:
Preserve visible food detail.

---

## Optional Accent

Small red accent may be used for specific products such as Inferno.

---

# 24. Environment

Preferred environment:

- Near-black background
- Dark reflective or matte floor
- Subtle atmospheric fog
- Warm golden highlights
- Small particles
- Minimal futuristic geometry

Avoid building an unnecessarily large 3D restaurant environment in Version 1.

---

# 25. Burger Material Direction

## Bun

- Warm brown/orange
- Soft highlights
- High roughness
- Fine bread texture

## Patty

- Dark brown
- Rough surface
- Irregular cooked texture

## Cheese

- Cheezish Gold direction
- Glossy
- Soft
- Melted edges

## Sauce

- Glossy
- Wet appearance
- Controlled reflections

## Pickles

- Green
- Slight wet/translucent appearance

Materials should avoid a plastic appearance.

---

# 26. Particle Style

Particles should feel connected to food and Cheezish.

Allowed ideas:

- Sesame seeds
- Cheese crumbs
- Golden dust
- Small ingredient fragments
- Subtle steam/smoke particles

Avoid:

- Random stars
- Sci-fi spark overload
- Constant particle noise

---

# 27. Motion Language

Animations should feel:

- Smooth
- Heavy
- Controlled
- Cinematic

Avoid:

- Bouncy animations
- Random spinning
- Flashing
- Excessive movement
- Animation without purpose

---

# 28. Motion Timing

Suggested UI transitions:

150ms–300ms

Suggested section reveals:

600ms–1200ms

Suggested cinematic transitions:

1.2s–2.5s

Scroll-controlled 3D scenes may use scrubbed timelines.

These values are starting guidelines and may be adjusted after browser testing.

---

# 29. Animation Intensity

## High Motion

Use for:

- Particle intro
- Hero
- Ingredient explosion
- Final order CTA

## Medium Motion

Use for:

- Signature burger showcase
- Gallery

## Low Motion

Use for:

- Menu
- About
- Contact

Do not animate every element simply because animation is available.

---

# 30. Reduced Motion Design

When prefers-reduced-motion is enabled:

Reduce or remove:

- Camera fly-through
- Aggressive object rotation
- Long particle transitions
- Complex parallax
- Scroll-controlled object travel

Keep:

- Burger visual
- Text
- Navigation
- Menu
- Buttons
- Core page structure

The website must remain fully understandable.

---

# 31. HUD / Ingredient Labels

Small futuristic labels may appear around the 3D burger.

Example:

01 / DOUBLE SMASH
100% BEEF

Possible style:

- Small uppercase text
- Thin lines
- Warm cream text
- Gold number/accent

These labels should remain secondary to the food.

---

# 32. Forms

Preferred form style:

Dark minimal interface.

Fields:

NAME
EMAIL
PHONE
GUESTS
DATE
TIME

Style:

- Clear visible label
- Dark background
- Simple underline or border
- Gold active/focus state
- Clear error message

Placeholder text must not replace actual labels.

---

# 33. Icons

Use simple line-based icons.

Possible icons:

- Arrow
- Menu
- Close
- Phone
- Location
- External link

Avoid:

- Random colorful icon sets
- Multiple conflicting icon styles

---

# 34. Mobile Design Principles

Mobile is not a compressed desktop design.

Mobile should use:

- Vertical layouts
- Large touch targets
- Simplified 3D
- Reduced particle count
- Reduced camera movement
- Strong Order Now CTA
- Clear typography
- Comfortable spacing

---

# 35. Desktop Design Principles

Desktop may use:

- Large typography
- Full cinematic composition
- Layered 3D
- More whitespace
- Richer transitions
- Horizontal gallery interaction
- Full burger explosion sequence

---
